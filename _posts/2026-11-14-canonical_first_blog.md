Welcome!!

It is a canon event that I make a first blog the day my website becomes active about the process of making the website. So that is what I am going to do.

I've been meaning to make a website for a while, but never found myself the time nor the motivation to😭. It wasn't until early in my sophomore year that i realized having a place to document my work would be nice, and having a place to post blogs that anbody online could find and read would be fun. 

I started by collecting around 50 personal websites of everybody i could find within 2 or 3 degrees of separation from me.

😁

Anyway, after getting enough inspo, I realized I seemed to have a rough layout of what i wanted it to look like, but no idea how to do it. For its simplicity, I structured my site based on [Nevin Thinagar](https://intentionally-aimless.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}'s blog. It is hosted on github pages from a personal repo, uses jekyll for styling and themes, and uses cloudinary for hosting images and videos. I found the [Minimal Mistakes](https://mmistakes.github.io/minimal-mistakes/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} jekyll theme that seemed to have just the right amount of structured features as well as flexibility for customizing. 

Writing documentation was a long process, but at least I felt like I was making progress while working through it. Tweaking the formatting for the site, however, was painful. It is really hard, especially working off the Minimal Mistakes theme, to not make it too "corporate blog" feeling. I enjoy websites that retain their level of humanity while still looking pretty, and it's hard to strike that balance.

I knew that I wanted Helvetica to be the typeface across my page, but unfortunately, Windows does not have Helvetica as a pre-installed system font. I ended up using [Andrew Sun](https://sunandrew.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}'s (really good TouchDesigner artist) elegant solution of loading Geist, and applying some custom CSS formatting to make it look nicer on their website. Huge appreciation to them!

Eventually, I landed on a format and layout that I was happy with...and will continue to iterate on.

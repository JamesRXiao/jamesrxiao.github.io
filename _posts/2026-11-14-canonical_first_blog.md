welcome to my page!

it is a canon event that i make a first blog the day my website becomes active about the process of making the website. so that is what i am going to do.

i've been meaning to make a website for a while, but never found myself the time nor the motivation to. it wasn't until early in my sophomore year that i realize having a place to document my work would be nice, and having a place to post blogs that anbody online could find and read would be fun. 

i started by collecting around 50 personal websites of everybody i could find within 2 or 3 degrees of separation from me. 

anyway, after getting enough inspo, i 



structured my site based on [nevin thinagar](https://intentionally-aimless.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}'s site. it is hosted on github pages from a personal repo, uses jekyll for styling and themes, and uses cloudinary for hosting images and videos. i found the [minimal mistakes](https://mmistakes.github.io/minimal-mistakes/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} jekyll theme that seemed to have just the right amount of structured features as well as flexibility for customizing.
